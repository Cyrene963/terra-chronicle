# Terra Chronicle 第二批资产视觉 Brief 与生成提示词

这份文档承接 `docs/second-wave-asset-families.md`，用于把第二批资产推进到可制作状态。

## 1. `ui_dungeon_node_combat`

### 视觉 Brief
地城战斗节点应像一枚镶在暗色羊皮地图上的战斗徽章。它必须让玩家一眼知道“这里会打架”，但不能像普通网页按钮。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle dungeon combat node icon, warm fantasy map UI, bronze ring, parchment map background, small crossed organic blades and vine thorns, subtle golden glow, dark dungeon atmosphere, premium roguelike map node, readable at small size, handcrafted UI, not emoji, not flat web icon, not neon.

### 负面词
flat icon, emoji, cyberpunk, plastic button, generic sword icon, sci-fi glow

## 2. `status_shield`

### 视觉 Brief
护甲图标要能在 24px 仍读清楚，是战斗 HUD 的基础状态符号。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle shield status icon, small readable fantasy HUD icon, root-and-bark shield shape, bronze rim, soft blue-gray protection glow, parchment UI compatible, clean silhouette, premium game status icon, readable at 24px, not emoji, not flat generic shield.

### 负面词
emoji, flat blue app icon, sci-fi shield, over-detailed emblem, unreadable tiny details

## 3. `crop_moon_turnip`

### 视觉 Brief
月萝是根茎作物的首发代表。它应当有明显的地下块根感，同时带一点月光/夜间魔力，适合恢复或炼金用途。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle fantasy root crop called moon turnip, round pale root vegetable with soft moonlit glow, small green leaves, earthy soil at base, harvestable and charming, warm fantasy farming game crop, clear seedling and mature stage potential, parchment UI compatible, not realistic stock vegetable, not toy-like cartoon.

### 负面词
stock photo turnip, hyperreal vegetable, plastic toy crop, generic farming icon

## 4. `monster_spore_gnarl`

### 视觉 Brief
孢瘤是菌群怪物的基础代表，要像菌类和枯木长在一起形成的怪物。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle spore gnarled monster, fungus and twisted root growth fused together, swollen spore sacs, cracked bark texture, gray-green and muted purple palette, misty fungal spores, readable silhouette, dungeon ecology monster, threatening but not gory, not undead, not demon, premium fantasy creature design.

### 负面词
zombie, skeleton, demon, blood gore, sci-fi alien, generic mushroom mascot

## 5. `beast_forge_ember`

### 视觉 Brief
炉火兽是火系灵兽的基础代表，要像会守着熔炉工作的温暖灵兽，不是普通火怪。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle fire spirit beast called forge ember, small helpful farm-and-workshop creature, warm copper and ember palette, glowing core in chest or horns, ash-tipped fur, gentle but energetic expression, designed for a fantasy farming workshop, functional pet, premium game creature design, not a generic fire elemental, not aggressive monster.

### 负面词
fire demon, dragon, generic flame monster, baby toy mascot, sci-fi reactor creature

## 6. `material_wood_raw`

### 视觉 Brief
原木是基础材料，但也必须有 Terra 的手工感，适合库存格和工坊 UI。

### 主提示词
A Terra Chronicle raw wood material icon, small bundle of cut logs, warm brown wood grain, slight moss and bark texture, clean readable inventory icon, parchment UI compatible, handcrafted fantasy farming material, simple but premium, not realistic photo, not flat app icon.

### 负面词
photo log, flat icon, plastic toy, messy pile, unreadable tiny detail

## 7. 第二批统一补充词

所有第二批资产可追加：
- Terra Chronicle
- warm fantasy farming game
- parchment and bronze UI compatible
- handcrafted premium game asset
- clear silhouette
- readable at game UI size

## 8. 执行建议

第二批不要一次性生成几十张。先每个类别生成 2-4 个候选，做 reference board，再选统一方向批量扩展。
